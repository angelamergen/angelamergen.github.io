E-Newsletter Creation and Management: Alumni Greetings

Media Type: Newsletter (Writing, Communications, Project Management)

Tools: Razor’s Edge Email Builder, Canva, Google Docs, Zoom, Google Meet, Email

Project Date: Fall of 2023 - Spring of 2026

Project Snapshot:

The Alumni Greetings E-Newsletter is a monthly digital publication sent via email to alumni, current students, faculty, staff, donors, and board members of Trinity. Over several years, I managed the Alumni Greetings production cycle, refreshed its creative layout, and wrote alumni testimonials that reflected Trinity’s mission and community. This project used skills in writing, interviewing, content design, and audience engagement.

Media Type: Newsletter (Writing, Communications, Project Management)

Tools: Razor’s Edge Email Builder, Canva, Google Docs, Zoom, Google Meet, Email

Project Date: Fall of 2023 - Spring of 2026

My Role:

I assisted in the management and creation of the Alumni Greetings monthly E-Newsletter. This included coordinating with alumni, interviewing, writing their stories, and preparing content in the email builder including pictures and graphics. Over time, I updated layouts to align better with Trinity’s branding, and contributed to both the storytelling quality and visual consistency of the newsletter.

Goals

Highlight a diverse amount of alumni - with variety in generations and vocations.

Continuously communicate Trinity's missions and impact through real life examples.

Maintain a consistent monthly newsletter production.

Improve design quality and brand alignment.

Track engagement metrics.

The Process

The management and creation of the Alumni Greetings took much time and thought from month to month.

Select and contact alumni for the monthly spotlight.

Schedule and conduct interviews, or collect written articles.

Write and revise the alumni stories.

Prepare content in the email builder, creating a clean layout.

Edit and prepare photos to match branding and maintain consistency.

Send test emails, finalize format, schedule the send.

Monitor open rate and engagement to evaluate performance.

Design and Writing Approach

Over the years, I refreshed the designed layout of the Alumni Greetings with a more branded newsletter. I incorporated more college colors and created simple design elements like photo overlays in Canva to create a seamless look.

Writing alumni stories required thought, active listening, and clear communication. For every edition, I focused on the “so what” from the alumnus’ story; this was the unique insight or encouragement that readers could take away. My goal was to represent each alum honestly while staying aligned with Trinity’s mission and purpose.
