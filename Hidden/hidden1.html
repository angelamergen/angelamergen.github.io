Angela’s Portfolio Voice Guide

As I’ve been building my website and refining my professional presence, I realized how helpful it is to have a clear, consistent voice—one that reflects both who I am and the kind of work I want to create. So I put together my own Brand Voice Guide, a tool I’ll use as I write, design, and share new projects. I’m posting it here not only for fun, but also as a behind-the-scenes look at the tone, style, and purpose that shape my work. Whether you’re a fellow creative, a potential employer, or someone just browsing, I hope this gives you a better sense of my approach and the heart behind what I do.

Voice Pillars

Joyful and purpose-driven

  • Do: use a warm tone, uplifting language, express emotional warmth
  • Don’t: use overly spiritual jargon

Personable (friendly and encouraging)

  • Do: be friendly, uplifting, and professional
  • Don’t: sound too casual or unprofessional

Confident and direct

  • Do: use language plainly and naturally, write concise and purposeful sentences, introductions that get to the point
  • Don’t: use very flowery and overly sophisticated language (like too many adjectives)

Audience Snapshot

My audience is to businesses (both employers and clients) in need of marketing or business administration. I focus on small to medium-sized businesses! I bring creativity, reliability, and a team-oriented attitude to the workspace.. I am able and interested in doing a variety of marketing and organizational support tasks, such as: designing, digital marketing, social media, content writing, radio broadcasting, and office management.

Identify Tone in Different Pages

  • About Page: joyful, purposeful, and confident
  • Portfolio projects: clear, purposeful
  • Contact Page: direct and inviting

Style Moves

Use: action verbs, specific examples, concise language

Avoid: flowery language, buzz words, m-dashes, long introductions

Bio Trio

1 Sentence (25–35 words)

A creative marketer driven by curiosity and purpose. I craft meaningful content that connects with people and supports small and midsize businesses.

Short (60–80 words)

I am a creative marketer passionate about helping small and midsize businesses communicate with clarity, purpose, and heart. With a people-first mindset and a natural enthusiasm for problem-solving, I enjoy crafting content and organizing systems that make brands feel both approachable and confident. My work is grounded in joy, curiosity, and a desire to serve others well—bringing fresh ideas, steady reliability, and a positive presence to every project I take on.

Medium (120–150 words)

I’m a marketer who finds genuine joy in helping businesses share their story with clarity and confidence. I love creating content, building organized systems, and developing ideas that help small and midsize organizations connect more meaningfully with the people they serve. My work is grounded in purpose—shaped by both my Christian faith and my desire to encourage others through what I create. I bring a blend of creativity, dependability, and warmth to every environment, aiming to make collaboration enjoyable and communication clear. Above all, I strive to produce work that not only looks good but genuinely supports the mission and heart behind each organization.
